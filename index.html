Victor Garcia

Food Pantry Organizer📚

My name is Victor Garcia. I’m a hard-working individual with a strong interest in art, learning, teamwork, and solving problems. I learned much of what I know through small, meaningful experiences that gave me the drive to work effectively. Although I gained a lot of social experience through the school system, I attribute more of my work ethic to my family. My uncles work as construction workers; hard-working people surrounded me. However, the work I started training for as I entered adulthood involved talking and communicating with customers. I volunteered at a kids' club, supervised children's safety, and answered their questions. This work led to my first paid job as a teacher's assistant at an elementary school. Later, I worked as a food pantry organizer for a food bank, where I learned to follow instructions, guide coworkers to assigned tasks, and work in a team setting. These experiences shaped my work ethic and taught me to stay calm, patient, efficient, and collaborative. With a focus on environmental engineering, I can adapt to the work while bringing ideas to solve climate issues. It calls for the kind of labor I’m willing to put in. Many of my jobs required hard work, but also communication, sharing, and collaboration. I take on hard work and can bring a sense of finality. As a pantry organizer, I learned to engage with customers and keep individuals calm and comfortable in the food bank. Working there gave me a deeper understanding of how to categorize food products and safely organize tools so they stay out of the way of customers and workers. I will use these skills to sort and analyze the many participants who will submit their headshots and bios for me to choose from, while also incorporating my co-workers' input when given. Currently, I work as a Food Pantry Organizer, where I set up and distribute food for low-income families—collaborating with my co-workers and multitasking between organizing, restacking shelves, and listening to customers' needs. I have been in this job for around 5 months, building my skills and familiarity with food distribution, which will help me if I ever work in retail. I may not pursue this path, but it's a good plan for the future. A memory that shaped me into the person I am today is when my family and I helped my aunt and cousin, who lived in a two-story house, move out all her belongings. Working from day to dusk, I helped move furniture, sort through toys and paper, and clean and sweep the floors. All of this heavy labor was a huge accomplishment for me in my early teens, and it gave me valuable experience with difficult work. I expect my career will include many jobs where I can gain knowledge through physical labor. My biggest fear is being unprepared and lacking that knowledge, and I don’t intend to let that happen. I want to be a willing worker who can adapt; I'll keep that as my personal skill as a hard worker.
